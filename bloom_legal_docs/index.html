Frequently asked

Does Bloom send my data anywhere?

No servers. No analytics. No account. Everything you log lives on your iPhone and syncs privately to your own iCloud (Apple's servers, using end-to-end encryption for CloudKit private database). Bloom the app never sees your data.

Can Bloom read my Apple Pay transactions automatically?

Not directly — Apple doesn't let third-party apps read Wallet history. For Apple Card, Apple Cash, and Apple Savings we use Apple's FinanceKit (device-only). For every other card, Bloom guides you through a 2-minute iOS Shortcuts automation that pipes each tap-to-pay into the app.

How do I import a bank statement?

Settings → Data → Import CSV. Bloom auto-detects common bank column names, previews a few rows, and dedupes so re-importing the same statement is a no-op.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The local copy goes with the app. If you had iCloud sync on, the copy in your iCloud stays until you turn iCloud sync off for Bloom in Settings → iCloud → Bloom, or use Bloom's "Erase all data" first.

Is there a subscription?

No. One-time purchase, no ads, no in-app upsells.

Can I use Bloom on my iPad or Mac?

Bloom is iPhone-first today. iPad and Mac versions may come later.
