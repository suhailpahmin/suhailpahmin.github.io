1. What Bloom is

Bloom is an iOS app that helps you record and organize your personal finances — transactions, accounts, cards, budgets, savings goals, and recurring commitments. It runs on your device, with optional private iCloud sync between your own devices. There is no Bloom server; there is no Bloom account.

Bloom is not financial advice, tax advice, investment advice, or a substitute for a licensed advisor. Any suggestions, insights, or "advisor" narrations inside the app are informational summaries of numbers you entered — not recommendations. Use your own judgment for real financial decisions.

  • Use the built-in Export all data feature (Settings → Data) to keep a portable copy.
  • If you factory-reset your device, sign out of iCloud, or uninstall the app, your local data may be lost. iCloud copies remain under your Apple ID's control.
