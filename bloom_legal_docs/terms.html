Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version. Bloom is a personal-finance tracker you run on your iPhone. Use it to organize your money. It's not financial advice. Take reasonable care of your data — Bloom can't recover it for you.

1. What Bloom is

Bloom is an iOS app that helps you record and organize your personal finances — transactions, accounts, cards, loans, installments, bills, budgets, and savings goals. It runs on your device, with optional private iCloud sync between your own devices. There is no Bloom server; there is no Bloom account.

2. What Bloom is not

Bloom is not financial advice, tax advice, investment advice, or a substitute for a licensed advisor. Any suggestions, insights, advisor summaries, or AI-generated narrations inside the app are informational summaries of numbers you entered — not recommendations. Use your own judgment for real financial decisions.

Bloom's card-payment feature records a payment you made (or plan to make) so your balances stay accurate. It does not actually move money or communicate with your bank. Real payments happen in your banking app or branch.

3. Your data is your responsibility

  • You keep the only copy that matters — on your device, and (if enabled) in your iCloud.
  • Bloom has no server-side backup and cannot restore data you delete.
  • Use the built-in Export all data feature (Settings → Data) to keep a portable copy.
  • If you factory-reset your device, sign out of iCloud, or uninstall the app, your local data may be lost. iCloud copies remain under your Apple ID's control.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Bloom Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or annual) through Apple's App Store. Here's the plain version:

  • Billing. Apple charges your Apple ID at the price shown at purchase. Prices may vary by region and may change; Apple will tell you before any price increase takes effect.
  • Auto-renewal. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • Cancel any time. Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions → Bloom. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep premium until the end of the current period.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Bloom cannot issue refunds directly.
  • Free trials, if offered, convert to a paid subscription unless you cancel before the trial ends.

6. Third-party services

Bloom relies on a few Apple platform frameworks and two small third-party SDKs:

  • iCloud / CloudKit — optional cross-device sync of your data (Apple).
  • FinanceKit — optional read of your Apple Card / Cash / Savings activity, on-device (Apple).
  • Shortcuts — optional Apple Pay / bank-notification auto-logging via automations you build (Apple).
  • Speech, Vision, Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence) — on-device voice-to-text, receipt OCR, and AI features (Apple).
  • Local Authentication — Face ID / passcode app-lock (Apple).
  • StoreKit — subscriptions and in-app purchases (Apple).
  • Sentry — crash and app-hang reports plus the Send-feedback form (third party; opt-out in Settings → Privacy).
  • Aptabase — anonymous usage counters (third party; opt-out in Settings → Privacy).

What the third-party SDKs collect and how to opt out is described in our Privacy Policy. Your use of all of the above is also governed by Apple's and the respective provider's terms.
