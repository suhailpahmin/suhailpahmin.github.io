One minute with a family that looks out for each other.

Shared care

Look out for Mom and Dad, from anywhere.

They share a code with you. You can see whether today's doses were taken and when something is running low, and send a gentle nudge. You never see their exact quantities, and they can stop sharing at any time.

Reminders

Take it, or snooze it, right from the reminder.

Mark a dose as taken or snooze it without opening the app. Trevane remembers, so you don't have to.

Refills

Refill before the bottle is empty.

Trevane counts what you have left and lets you know while there's still time to get more.

Adding medications

Add a medication in seconds.

Start typing and pick the right name from the list. No guessing at spellings.

Appointments

Ready for every appointment.

Share your medication list in one tap, so your doctor or pharmacist sees exactly what you take.
