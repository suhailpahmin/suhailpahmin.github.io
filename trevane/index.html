Reminders that don't give up

Set the times you take each medication. Trevane reminds you, and you can snooze a few more minutes if your hands are full. See what's coming next right from your home screen, or mark a dose taken just by asking.

  • Snooze for a few more minutes
  • Next dose on your home screen
  • Mark a dose taken by voice

Know before you run out

Turn on supply tracking and Trevane counts down what's left as you take each dose. It warns you while you're getting low — not the morning you take your last one.

  • Counts down as you take each dose
  • Warns you before you run out
  • Your choice of how much warning

Check in, don't surveil

Invite someone you trust with a short code. They can see whether doses have been taken and whether you're running low — never your exact quantities, never your full list, unless you choose to share more.

  • Invite with a short code
  • They see status, not quantities
  • Remove access whenever you like

A list you can hand over

Appointments go better when you can say exactly what you're taking. Share your medication list straight from the app, as a simple list you can send or hand over at the desk.

  • Real medication names, not guesses
  • Share in one tap

Privacy policy Support

Trevane is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, does not check drug interactions, and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor or pharmacist. Never start, stop or change a medication without speaking to your prescriber first.
