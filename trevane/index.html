Trevane

A calm medication reminder, with refill alerts before you run out and a simple way for someone you trust to check in.

  • No advertising, no ad trackers. Your medication list is never sold.
  • Use it on your own, or invite someone you trust with a short code to check in.

Privacy policy Support

Trevane is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, does not check drug interactions, and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor or pharmacist. Never start, stop or change a medication without speaking to your prescriber first.
