See Trevane in action

See that Mom took her medication.

Share a code with someone who looks out for you. They can see whether today's doses were taken and whether something is running low. They never see your exact quantities, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Reminders you can act on.

Mark a dose taken or snooze it, right from the reminder. No need to open the app first.

Know before you run out.

Trevane tracks what you have left and lets you know while there's still time to refill, not after the bottle's empty.

Real medication names.

Start typing and pick from the list. No guessing at spellings.

Ready for your next appointment.

Share your medication list in one tap, so your doctor or pharmacist sees exactly what you take.

No ads.
No advertising trackers.

Your medication list is never sold and never used for advertising. Add as many medications as you need.

Never wonder if it was taken.

Trevane keeps track of every dose, for you and for the people you look after.

Privacy policy Support

Trevane is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, does not check drug interactions, and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor or pharmacist. Never start, stop or change a medication without speaking to your prescriber first.
